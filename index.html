How a kitchen job runs

We come on site twice, once before the cabinets and once after the benchtops, then close the job with the paperwork.

STEP 1
Site visit and quote
We check the existing plumbing, go through the layout with you or your builder, and give one price for the whole plumbing scope.
STEP 2
Rough-in
Pipes and drainage go in to match the new layout, before cabinetry and wall linings.
STEP 3
Fit-off
After benchtops and splashbacks, we connect the sink, taps, dishwasher, fridge line and cooktop.
STEP 4
Inspection and certificate
The licensed plumber files the Notice of Work and the work is inspected. Work that meets the standards gets a certificate of compliance.

What NSW requires

All plumbing, drainage and gas work must be done by a licensed plumber, and passing work receives a certificate of compliance.
Kitchen, bathroom and laundry work valued over $5,000 needs a contractor licence, and the contract must be in writing.
Statutory warranties apply: six years for major defects and two years for everything else.
